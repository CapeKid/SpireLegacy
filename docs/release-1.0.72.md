# 1.0.72 — Distinct classes

Look-alike cards are differentiated, inside each class and across classes, by leaning on each class's mechanic. Rarities are rebalanced while keeping every class's Common/Uncommon/Rare counts.

## Arcane Flow

- Arcane Charge is no longer capped at 2. Unused charges dissipate at the end of your turn. Arcane Flow still triggers once per turn and draws one card per charge consumed.
- Effects that needed exactly 2 charges (Celestial Spear, Moonstone Lance, Starfall, Rune Detonation, Soulfire, Triune Crest) now trigger at 2 or more.
- Toxic Theory: whenever you apply a debuff to an enemy, gain 1 Arcane Charge. Upgrade costs 0.
- Infinite Script: Arcane Flow draws 1 additional card. Upgrade costs 0.
- Book of Embers (beta): costs 1; whenever you Exhaust a card, gain 1 Arcane Charge. Upgrade costs 0.

## Knight

- Sword Dance is Common and gains Retain. Banner Charge hits all enemies for 4.
- Riposte deals 7 and gives 4 Block next turn (6 upgraded).
- Patient Riposte deals 10, plus 6 if you have Block, and no longer grows while Retained.
- Rarity: Veteran Training is Rare; Meteor Hammer and Blood Price are Uncommon.

## Mage

- Magic Barrier gains 1 Arcane Charge instead of drawing.
- Witchfire deals 5, plus 4 after Arcane Flow has triggered, and no longer applies Poison.
- Starfall deals 5 ×3 to all enemies, plus 3 per hit with 2 or more charges.
- Moonstone Lance deals 5, plus 6 with 2 or more charges.
- Hex Rain deals 5 to all enemies and also applies 1 Vulnerable.
- Rarity: Spell Threads is Rare; Spell Surge is Uncommon.

## Ranger

- Double Shot costs 0 and deals 2 ×2. Scattershot deals 4 to all enemies and discards 1.
- Rapid Fire gains Retain and deals 1 extra per hit to Vulnerable or Poisoned targets.
- Storm of Arrows deals 3 ×4 to all enemies, plus 2 per hit to Vulnerable or Poisoned targets.
- Ricochet costs 2 and deals 3 ×4 to random enemies.
- Unseen Hunter (beta) costs 1: at the start of your turn, draw 2 cards, then discard 2.
- Rarity: Tailwind is Rare; Heartseeker and Snap Shot are Uncommon; Mark Prey is Common.

## Interface

- The Embark button in the heir picker shows a visible focus ring for controller and keyboard navigation.
