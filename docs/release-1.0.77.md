# 1.0.77 — Shared family core

- The 34 shared family cards keep their rarity split (8 common, 16 uncommon, 10 rare), now with 12 Attacks, 15 Skills, and 7 Powers.
- Retired from the shared core: Cold Read, Arms Vault, Spellweave, Forest Remedy, Master Plan, Burnished Legacy, Secret Thesis, Measured Breath, Alchemist's Patience, and Perfect Timing. Saves that contain them still load.
- New shared cards: Heirloom Blade, Banner Volley, Inherited Grudge, Lineage Strike, Heraldry, Hearth Memory, Old Grudges, Birthright, Crown Breaker, and Generations.
- Generations costs 3 energy, or 2 when upgraded.
- Clean Armor now grants 5 Block (8 upgraded), still removes debuffs, and no longer Exhausts.
- Family Motto, Heirloom Blade, Clean Armor, Banner Volley, Heraldry, and Birthright each grant an extra effect based on the heir's class. In combat, cards show only your class's bonus; the Compendium shows all three.
- Family Motto (0 energy: draw 1 card, 2 when upgraded) is a new shared starter card. Starting decks have 4 Strikes, 4 Guards, the class signature card, and Family Motto.
- All 11 new cards have their own illustrations.
