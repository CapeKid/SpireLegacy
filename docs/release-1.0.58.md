# 1.0.58 — Perch Discipline and Transmutation

- Perch Discipline grants 1 Energy next turn if you Retain at least one Attack. It costs 2 Energy, or 1 when upgraded.
- Transmutation lets you Exhaust up to two cards from your hand. Gain 1 Energy for each card Exhausted this way and 1 Arcane Charge if you Exhaust at least one. It costs 1 Energy, or 0 when upgraded, and Exhausts.
