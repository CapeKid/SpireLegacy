# 1.0.56 — Card balance

- Spellweave draws one card next turn. Reforge moved to Uncommon and draws one card at both levels. Clean Armor moved to Common.
- Common Weak cards start at one stack. Tripwire grants more Block, Chilling Words adds Scry, and Frost Ring, Hex Rain, Merciless, and Frozen Spear have revised damage and debuff scaling.
- Challenge deals more damage, Serpent Oil starts with more Poison, and Grand Melee hits five times with higher damage per hit.
- Hourglass discounts a chosen card by 2 this turn and gains Retain on upgrade. Forest Remedy adds 6 Block. On public-beta, Scouting now scries, draws two, then discards one.
- Snipe deals bonus damage to full-health enemies, Patient Riposte starts with more damage, and Soulfire applies Poison twice when its attack starts with two Arcane Charges and Arcane Flow ready.
