# 1.0.69 — Blitz traits and card roles

- In Blitz, Lootbox Addict offers a potion in treasure rooms, Charismatic gives one extra pick at the first Merchant, and Diva grants Vigor when enemies die. Blitz heir offers replace Spelunker, Butter Fingers, and Forgetful with compatible traits.
- All six Heir Ancient cards display their artwork at its original proportions.
- King's Ransom now trades HP for Strength and Dexterity. Reworked Mana Fountain, Thunderchain, Spring Nock, Razor Fletching, Venom Rune, and Perfect Ward to give each a distinct role.
