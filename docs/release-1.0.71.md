# 1.0.71 — Native card curve

Cards are retuned against native cards from every character, counting the manor's Strength and Dexterity. See "Manor-state difficulty targets" in `manor-and-balance.md`.

- Multi-hit cards no longer outscale native multi-hits once the manor is built: Sword Dance and Rapid Fire hit 3 times, Ricochet deals 2 per hit, and Volley deals 4 per hit.
- Mana Fountain and King's Ransom now cost 1. Heavy Harness gives 10 Block. Reinforce gives 4 Block now and 4 next turn.
- AoE and conditional attacks are raised toward native rates: Sweeping Edge and Scattershot deal 5, Hex Rain 6, Executioner Axe 11, and Finishing Flurry chains for 5. Long Reach deals 5 (8 upgraded) and applies 1 Weak, so it no longer duplicates Aim.
- Effects the manor does not scale are matched to native: Bulwark and Burnished Legacy give 3, Cruel Precision and Toxic Theory 3, Poison Tip 5 Poison, Barbed Arrow 2 Poison, Witchfire 4 damage and 2 Poison. Astral Mantle and Infinite Script now cost 1. Bastion grants 3 Dexterity.
- Unbroken Wall gives 24 Block (30 upgraded). Meditate draws 2 (3 upgraded). Memory Prism gains 3 Block (5 upgraded). Stored Ember and Crystal Seed start at 4 and grow by 3 per retain. Forest Remedy costs 1 and its upgrade adds 3 Block.
- The shop test harness supports both the regular merchant API and the beta's `MerchantSynchronizer`.
