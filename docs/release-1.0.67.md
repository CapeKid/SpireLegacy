# 1.0.67 — Fragile and Ancient rewards

- Max HP costs stop at 1 for Fragile heirs. Neow and Neow’s Bones skip Precarious Shears when its HP cost would be lethal.
- Continue restores 1 HP to affected saves stuck after Leafy Poultice, Precarious Shears, or Vakuu’s max HP reward. Ancient rewards no longer recreate a save after a defeat.
