# 1.0.66 — Heir picker and crown bonuses

- Trait crown bonuses add together in full, then Treasury multiplies the payout. Fragile gives +200%, and Fragile with Pacifist gives +350% before Treasury.
- The heir picker shows each trait’s bonus and the combined total. The compact layout fits all three offers at 1280×800.
- Pick your next heir when you embark. The Family Manor has upgrades, the Family Chronicle, and your active heir’s details during a climb.
