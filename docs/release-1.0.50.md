# 1.0.50 — Hollow Bones translucency

Hollow Bones now renders the character sprite at 75% opacity and no longer applies a 10% size reduction. This applies in combat, shops and rest sites, and composes with palettes, grayscale, rainbow effects and damage flashes. Banners, health bars, status icons and independent cosmetic accents retain their normal opacity. Other size traits still apply normally.

The trait retains its stable lowergravity ID, -12 maximum HP and +2 Dexterity. Its description now says translucent appearance. This build also includes the Methodical rename prepared in 1.0.49, whose Workshop publication was waiting for Steam sign-in.
