# 1.0.73 — Distinct cards and fair crowns

More look-alike cards are differentiated, and trait crown bonuses now match how much each trait helps or hurts. Values account for Manor Strength and Dexterity.

## Knight

- Twin Cut deals 2 ×2 and gains 2 Block.
- Pommel Knock deals 3, plus 2 per Skill played this turn (up to 3 Skills), and no longer applies Weak. Upgrade +2 damage.
- Tower Guard gains 5 Block, plus 3 if you already have Block, and no longer has Retain. Upgrade +2 Block.

## Ranger

- Rapid Fire no longer has Retain.
- Heartseeker deals 12 and applies 2 Vulnerable. Upgrade +3 damage.
- Sort Quiver draws 2, discards 1, and gains 1 Energy if you discarded an Attack. Upgraded draws 3.

## Trait crown bonuses

- Now free, because they are net upsides: Masochism, Vampirism and Dwarfism.
- Lowered: Inter-dimensional and Lootbox Addict +10%, Perfectionist and Crippling Intellect +20%, Diva +25%, Vegan +40%.
- Now pay crowns: Clumsy and IBS +15%, Overcompensation +25%.
- Raised: Puritan +30%, Forgetful +40%, Associative Agnosia +50%, Exhausted and Explosive Enemies +75%.
